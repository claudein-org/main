# Your Claude Code is now a social media manager

Writing for LinkedIn is a context switch. You leave your editor, fight a web
form, second-guess the wording, and lose the thread of whatever you were
actually building. **ClaudeIn** closes that gap: your posts live in a plain
`claudein.yml` file next to your code, and Claude Code can draft them for you.

## How it works

You keep one project folder. ClaudeIn watches it and streams a live preview to
your browser — the moment you save, the dashboard updates.

```bash
cin start
```

- **Write in your editor.** Posts are just YAML — text, images, video, or a
  Markdown article that points at a `.md` file in `articles/`.
- **Let Claude Code write.** Ask it to draft a post and it edits `claudein.yml`
  directly; the preview refreshes itself.
- **Publish in one click.** Connect LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and more, then
  ship straight from the preview.

## Why a file?

A file is something you, your editor, and your agent can all share. There's no
lock-in and no copy-paste dance — your brand and your backlog of posts are
versioned right alongside the work they're about.

That's the whole idea: **stay in flow, and let your tools handle LinkedIn.**
